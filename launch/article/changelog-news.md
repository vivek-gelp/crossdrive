## Submission (two sentences)

Claude's connectors see one Google account each, and the most-reacted open request on its connector tracker asks to change that; this write-up explains why it is structurally hard and compares four honest ways around it, from switching accounts by hand to a community Google Workspace MCP server to a small self-hosted server. It ends with what to insist on from any tool that lets an AI touch your files and mail, and with three real bugs found by testing on live accounts before release.

## Why it is newsworthy (for your own judgement, not for pasting)

- It is useful without installing anything: two of the four options need no new software, and the safety checklist applies to any tool.
- It documents a current, widely felt gap with sources dated November 2026 (an open request with 44 reactions, a Shared Drive bug with 22) and Google's rule that ownership cannot cross organisations.
- The testing section is a candid account of what broke before release and how it was fixed, which is the kind of story developers building MCP servers can learn from.
