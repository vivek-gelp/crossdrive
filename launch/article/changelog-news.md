## Submission (two sentences)

A practical guide to using several Google accounts with an AI assistant, updated on 13 November 2026 for user reports (not yet confirmed by Anthropic) that Claude's built-in Gmail and Drive connectors are gaining several accounts; it says when the built-in connector is enough and compares four honest options, from the built-in connector to a community Google Workspace MCP server to a small self-hosted server. It ends with what to insist on from any tool that lets an AI touch your files and mail, and with three real bugs found by testing on live accounts before release.

## Why it is newsworthy (for your own judgement, not for pasting)

- It is useful without installing anything: two of the four options need no new software, it tells readers to use the built-in connector when that is enough, and the safety checklist applies to any tool.
- It documents a widely felt gap with sources dated November 2026 (an open request with 44 reactions, a Shared Drive bug with 22), Google's rule that ownership cannot cross organisations, and a dated, clearly labelled update on the reported built-in change.
- The testing section is a candid account of what broke before release and how it was fixed, which is the kind of story developers building MCP servers can learn from.
