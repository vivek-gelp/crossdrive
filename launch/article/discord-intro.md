Hi all, I built Crossdrive, a self-hosted remote MCP server that puts every Google Drive and Gmail account under a label and enforces rules per account on the server (read-only, mail off, draft or send, trash-only delete, dry run, undo, an audit log), plus copy, move and search between accounts. I also wrote up four ways to work with several Google accounts with an AI assistant, including the built-in connector (there are unconfirmed reports it is gaining several accounts; if you have it and only read and draft, use it), plus what to insist on from any tool that touches your files: https://vivek-gelp.github.io/crossdrive/blog/several-google-accounts-with-claude.html It is MIT licensed, only the Claude desktop app has been verified as a client, OneDrive and Dropbox are still beta and untested live, and if you try it and get stuck I am happy to help here or in a GitHub issue.
