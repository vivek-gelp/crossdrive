## Two sentences

Crossdrive is a free, MIT-licensed, self-hosted MCP server that gives an AI assistant one connection onto several Google
Drive and Gmail accounts, with safety enforced in code: trash-only delete, draft-only mail by default, and read-only
accounts. I built it to solve my own multi-account problem and am sharing it in case it helps you; OneDrive and Dropbox
support is beta and not yet tested on live accounts.

## 60 words

Crossdrive is a free, MIT-licensed, self-hosted MCP server. It gives an AI assistant one connection onto several Google
Drive and Gmail accounts, each under a label you choose. Safety lives in code: trash is the only delete, mail is
draft-only by default, and any account can be read-only. OneDrive and Dropbox are beta, untested live. Feedback welcome.
