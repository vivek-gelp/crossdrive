# Short blurbs for directory listings

## One line (100 characters or fewer)

Self-hosted MCP server: every Google Drive and Gmail account under a label, with rules per account.

## Two sentences

Crossdrive is a free, MIT-licensed, self-hosted MCP server that puts every Google Drive and Gmail account under a label
you choose, enforces rules per account on the server (read-only, mail off, draft or send, trash-only delete, audit log)
and copies, moves and searches between accounts. I built it for my own accounts and am sharing it in case it helps you;
only the Claude desktop app has been verified as a client, and OneDrive and Dropbox support is beta and untested live.

## 60 words

Crossdrive is a free, MIT-licensed, self-hosted MCP server. Every Google Drive and Gmail account sits under a label you
choose, and the server enforces rules per account: read-only, mail off, draft or send, trash-only delete, dry run, undo,
audit log. It copies, moves and searches between accounts. Tokens stay in your storage. OneDrive and Dropbox are beta.
